About

I'm a graphic designer with a passion for visual communication, editorial design, and branding. My work sits at the intersection of concept-driven thinking and craft — from riso-printed animations to full brand identities.

Background

Originally from Thailand, I bring a cross-cultural perspective to every project. Whether working with print, motion, or digital interfaces, I try to build work that feels both considered and alive.
