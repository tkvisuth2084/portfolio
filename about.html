About

I'm TK, a recent graduate from Boston University with a degree in Graphic Design and a minor in Computer Science. I'm interested in creating work that sits between design, storytelling, and technology. I enjoy exploring ideas through a mix of research, experimentation, and making!

Background

Growing up in Thailand and studying in the United States has shaped the way I see the world and approach design. I'm often inspired by cultural traditions, everyday observations, and systems that people interact with without noticing. Through print, motion, and digital work, I try to create projects that are thoughtful, curious, and engaging.
