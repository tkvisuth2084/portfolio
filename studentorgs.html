Student Organizations

Alongside my studies at Boston University, I led design for two student organizations. As Graphic Design Chair for BU THAISA, the Thai Student Association, I designed promotion for cultural events like Thai Night, along with event recaps and merchandise. As Marketing Chair for BU NMDP, I created flyers and social posts for general meetings and fundraisers, including the sponsor-backed Spin the Wheel.
